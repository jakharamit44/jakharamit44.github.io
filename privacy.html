Privacy Policy — Brainzap YouTube Uploader

Last updated: 29 October 2026.

Brainzap YouTube Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Brainzap Daily YouTube channel (youtube.com/@brainzaphindi). It is not offered to the public and it does not collect data from any other person.

Google user data accessed

Through the YouTube Data API, the tool accesses only what is needed to publish the owner's own videos:

  • Upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Add uploaded videos to the owner's own "Brainzap Daily Shorts" playlist.
  • Read basic upload status so the tool can confirm a video published successfully.

How data is stored and used

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own server and are used solely to upload the owner's own videos. They are never sold, shared, or sent to any third party.
  • No Google user data is used for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than publishing the owner's own videos.
  • No data about anyone other than the channel owner is accessed, stored, or processed.

Retention and revocation

Tokens are kept only while the tool is in use. The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account permissions page (Google Account > Security > Third-party access), after which the tool can no longer access the channel.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Brainzap Daily YouTube channel linked above.
